Enrollment & Admissions

Admissions at OneSkills Academy are subject to seat availability in small cohorts (capped at a maximum of 12 students per batch to ensure dedicated 1-on-1 mentor guidance). Seat reservation is confirmed upon registration and payment verification.

Free Demo Classes

Demo classes are 100% free with zero financial obligation. Prospective students are welcomed into our live campus sessions to experience mentor teaching style, inspect our modern learning facility, and review course syllabus before paying any fees.

Refund Policy

OneSkills Academy offers a 14-day full refund policy for all on-campus cohorts. Students may request a full refund within 14 calendar days of their fee payment date. Refund requests must be submitted in writing via email to info@oneskills.pk or through the campus admissions desk. Upon written receipt within the 14-day period, the tuition fee will be refunded in full.

Course Completion Certificates

Official Course Completion Certificates are awarded to students who complete the practical coursework and successfully defend their capstone portfolio project.

Lifetime Support & Alumni Network

Enrolled students and graduates receive lifetime access to our mentor help desk, dedicated alumni discussion channels, ongoing mentor guidance, and syllabus update consultations at no additional cost.
