Complete Syllabus Architecture

12 Weeks · 36 On-Campus Sessions · 84 Practical Lessons

Week 1

Digital Marketing Ecosystem, Consumer Psychology & Brand Positioning

3 Sessions · 7 Lessons
  • Deconstructing the digital sales funnel: Top of Funnel (Awareness), Middle (Consideration), Bottom (Purchase).
  • Buyer personas and market research for both local Pakistani businesses and international Western buyers.
  • Competitor advertising audit: dissecting active competitor campaigns using Meta Ad Library and Google Transparency Center.
Tools: Meta Ad Library, Google Trends, ChatGPT Research Prompts Practical Project: Conduct a full competitor audit for a local business
Week 2

Visual Asset Production with Canva Magic Studio & AI Media Generators

3 Sessions · 7 Lessons
  • Graphic design foundations: typography hierarchy, color balance, aspect ratios for Feed (1:1), Stories/Reels (9:16), and Display (16:9).
  • Canva Magic Studio workflows: Brand Kits, Magic Switch, background removal, and professional mockup generation.
  • Generating custom commercial ad imagery and realistic stock visuals using Midjourney & Ideogram.
Tools: Canva Pro, Midjourney, Ideogram, Remove.bg Practical Project: Design a 6-banner promotional asset kit for a client brand
Week 3

Direct-Response Copywriting with ChatGPT, Claude & Video Ads in CapCut

3 Sessions · 7 Lessons
  • Proven copywriting frameworks: AIDA (Attention, Interest, Desire, Action) and PAS (Problem, Agitation, Solution).
  • Advanced prompting architectures in ChatGPT and Claude for high-converting headline generation and hook variations.
  • Editing viral short-form video ads for TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts using CapCut AI captioning.
Tools: ChatGPT-4o, Claude 3.5 Sonnet, CapCut AI, ElevenLabs Practical Project: Produce 3 complete video ad variations with animated captions
Week 4

Meta Ads Mastery: Business Manager, Pixel Setup & Audience Targeting

3 Sessions · 7 Lessons
  • Setting up Meta Business Portfolio, 2FA security, ad accounts, and partner permissions.
  • Installing Meta Pixel and Conversions API (CAPI) on WordPress and Shopify stores with standard event tracking.
  • Broad targeting, detailed interest targeting, Lookalike Audiences (LAL), and custom retargeting lists.
Tools: Meta Business Suite, Events Manager, Meta Pixel Helper Practical Project: Configure a live Business Portfolio and install verified event tracking
Week 5

Meta Campaign Execution, CBO/ABO Budgets & Conversion Optimization

3 Sessions · 7 Lessons
  • Advantage+ Campaign Budget (CBO) vs. Ad Set Budget (ABO): when to test creatives and when to scale spend.
  • Launching Lead Generation campaigns with instant native forms for real estate, clinics, and education institutes.
  • E-commerce Purchase campaigns: Dynamic Product Ads (DPA), catalog sales, and scaling winning ad sets without fatigue.
Tools: Meta Ads Manager, Google Tag Manager Practical Project: Launch a live test campaign with A/B creative testing
Week 6

Google Search Ads, High-Intent Keyword Bidding & Local SEO

3 Sessions · 7 Lessons
  • Setting up Google Ads accounts, keyword match types (Exact, Phrase, Broad), and negative keyword pruning.
  • Writing high Quality Score responsive search ads with dynamic keyword insertion and ad assets (sitelinks, callouts).
  • Local SEO: Google Business Profile (GBP) verification, geo-tagged optimization, local review velocity, and citations.
Tools: Google Ads Manager, Google Keyword Planner, Google Business Profile Practical Project: Build a complete Google Search ad campaign and GBP audit
Week 7

Technical SEO, On-Page Optimization & Generative Engine Search (GEO/AEO)

3 Sessions · 7 Lessons
  • Technical SEO essentials: site speed, mobile responsiveness, XML sitemaps, robots.txt, and canonical tags.
  • On-page content optimization: H1-H3 structure, entity keyword integration, schema markup (LocalBusiness, FAQPage, Course).
  • Generative Engine Optimization (GEO): making brands citeable by AI engines like Perplexity, ChatGPT, and Google AI Overviews.
Tools: Google Search Console, Screaming Frog, RankMath, Schema Validator Practical Project: Complete an on-page and technical SEO overhaul on a live website
Week 8

Upwork Mastery: Profile Architecture, Specialized Profiles & Project Catalog

3 Sessions · 7 Lessons
  • Building a 100% completed Upwork profile that passes immediate manual approval.
  • Creating high-converting Specialized Profiles for Meta Ads Management and Local SEO.
  • Developing Upwork Project Catalog gigs: fixed-price service packages that generate inbound client orders without bidding connects.
Platform: Upwork.com Practical Project: Build and publish a live Upwork Profile with approved Project Catalog
Week 9

High-Ticket Proposal Bidding, Job Filtering & Client Interview Closing

3 Sessions · 7 Lessons
  • Job post forensics: identifying verified payment methods, high hire-rate clients, and avoiding lowball budget traps.
  • The 3-paragraph winning proposal formula: customized first lines, proof of competence, and low-friction call-to-action.
  • Loom video proposals: recording 90-second customized screen-share audits that achieve 4x interview conversion rates.
Tools: Upwork Proposal Scripts, Loom Screen Recorder Practical Project: Submit 5 verified live proposal bids with recorded Loom video audits
Week 10

Fiverr Gig SEO, Video Thumbnails & Rapid Review Velocity

3 Sessions · 7 Lessons
  • Fiverr search algorithm optimization: keyword-rich titles, URL permalinks, search tags, and FAQ indexing.
  • Designing clickable high-CTR gig images and producing compelling 30-second gig intro videos.
  • Structuring 3-tier pricing (Basic, Standard, Premium) and upselling gig extras to maximize Average Order Value.
Platform: Fiverr.com Practical Project: Create and publish 2 optimized Fiverr Gigs with video intros
Week 11

Local Client Acquisition: Pitching Retainers in Sheikhupura & Punjab

3 Sessions · 7 Lessons
  • Prospecting local commercial businesses: retail showrooms, doctors/clinics, private schools, restaurants, and manufacturers.
  • The 1-Page Client Proposal: presenting clear deliverables, expected ROAS/leads, and transparent retainer contracts.
  • Handling local client objections: contract agreements, advance payment policies, and ongoing monthly reporting.
Deliverables: Retainer Proposal Template, Client Onboarding Agreement Practical Project: Pitch a real local marketing audit to a prospective Sheikhupura business

USD Banking, Payoneer, Tax Documentation & Internship Transition

Frequently Answered Questions

Everything You Need to Know Before Enrolling

Can I learn Digital Marketing and Freelancing if I have no technical background?

Yes. This course is beginner-friendly. You do not need coding skills or an IT degree. We guide you step-by-step from content design in Canva and AI copywriting with ChatGPT to setting up Meta ads and launching client profiles on Upwork and Fiverr.

How does this course help me earn through both local and international clients?

You learn two distinct monetization channels: 1) International freelancing in USD on Upwork and Fiverr for high-ticket projects; and 2) Monthly marketing retainers (PKR 30,000 to PKR 80,000/month per client) managing social media, Google Maps, and Meta ads for local businesses in Sheikhupura and across Pakistan.

Do I need to bring my own laptop for the classes?

Yes. Students must bring their own laptops. OneSkills Academy does not provide individual student laptops or workstations. All hands-on exercises, campaign setups, and portfolio tasks are executed directly on your personal machine under mentor supervision.

Is there an internship opportunity after completing the course?

Yes. After completing the course, students can gain practical experience through a one-month unpaid internship. The internship period may be extended up to three months, depending on the available work and learning opportunities.

What modern AI marketing tools are taught in this course?

You will master ChatGPT and Claude for direct-response ad copywriting, customer research, and SEO briefs; Canva Magic Studio and Midjourney for visual creatives and ad banners; and CapCut AI for editing viral Reels, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts video ads.

How do Pakistani students receive payments from international clients?

We provide hands-on setup for Payoneer and Wise accounts linked directly to your local Pakistani bank account (IBAN) with proper tax documentation for legal foreign remittance clearance, as well as JazzCash, EasyPaisa, and bank transfers for local clients.

What is the tuition fee and refund guarantee policy?

Tuition is PKR 28,000 for the complete 12-week (3-month) on-campus program with flexible payment options (2 installments of PKR 14,000). Students may request a full refund within 14 calendar days of their fee payment date.
