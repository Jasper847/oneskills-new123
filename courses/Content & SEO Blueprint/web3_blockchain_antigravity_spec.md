## 10. Section 08: Course-Specific FAQ Accordion (8 High-Intent Questions)

1. **What is Airdrop Farming, and do I need a lot of money to start?**
   Airdrop farming is the process of testing and using early-stage Web3 protocols, blockchains, and dApps before they launch their official token—so you can qualify for potential early-user token distributions. You do **not** need large capital to start: we teach **Free Testnet Airdrops** and **Social Farming / Community Quests** (which require $0 in crypto) alongside low-gas Layer-2 Mainnet strategies.
2. **Do I need coding or programming skills to learn Web3 & Blockchain in this course?**
   No coding is required at all. While software engineers build smart contracts, this course trains you as an **on-chain Web3 practitioner and researcher**—teaching you how to use wallets, DEXs, bridges, DeFi protocols, block explorers, and airdrop platforms safely.
3. **Why are classes held 5 days a week for this program?**
   Web3 moves fast, and practical on-chain tasks (like setting up wallets, claiming testnet faucets, bridging across Layer-2 networks, and completing Galxe/Discord quests) are best learned by doing them live in class. Our **5 sessions per week (3:00–5:00 PM or 6:00–8:00 PM)** give you **80 full hours (67 lessons)** of hands-on lab practice with Bilal Ashraf and Webio.
4. **How do you help students avoid scams, wallet drainers, and fake airdrops?**
   Wallet security is the backbone of our curriculum (Modules 02, 10, 11, and 12). You will learn the **3-Wallet Isolation Rule** (separating your Vault, Active, and Burner wallets), how to read transaction simulations in Rabby Wallet, how to verify official project links on CryptoRank/DefiLlama, and how to revoke malicious smart contract approvals using **Revoke.cash**.
5. **What is "Sybil Detection" in airdrop farming, and is it covered?**
