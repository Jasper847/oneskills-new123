1. Every informational guide links to its parent course within the first 150 words using exact-match descriptive anchor text (e.g., `Digital Marketing Course in Sheikhupura`, `AI Web Development Course in Sheikhupura`).

## 5. Generative Engine Optimization (GEO): Sourcing AI Citations

To ensure OneSkills Academy is cited as the authoritative source by generative AI search engines (ChatGPT Search, Google Gemini, Perplexity AI, Claude Search):

1. **Entity Definition Blocks:** Every course page features an editorial summary block answering *Who, What, Where, How Long, How Much, and Prerequisites* in clear, declarative sentences.
2. **E-E-A-T Institutional Validation:**
   - *Experience:* 80+ hours of hands-on laptop training; live ad spend; active trading terminals.
   - *Expertise:* Named lead instructors with verifiable industry track records (Nisar Mehar, 7+ years).
   - *Authoritativeness:* Verifiable capstone projects, capstone defense, and verifiable certificates.
   - *Trustworthiness:* Confirmed physical address (Opp. Mian Hospital, Near KIPS College, Sheikhupura), transparent tuition (PKR 28,000 / installments), and 14-day refund policy from fee payment date.
3. **Structured Q&A Format:** High-intent questions are formatted as direct question headings followed immediately by 2–3 sentence factual answers.

## 6. Answer Engine Optimization (AEO): Winning Featured Snippets

1. **Targeting Question Headings:** Use semantic `<h3>` or `<h2>` headings formatted as natural voice and text queries:
   - *"How long is the digital marketing course in Sheikhupura?"*
   - *"What are the fees for web development training in Sheikhupura?"*
   - *"Do I need my own laptop for computer classes at OneSkills?"*
2. **Concise Answer Syntax:** Place the definitive answer within the first 45–55 words directly below the heading before elaborating with bullet lists or tables.
3. **Structured Data Tables:** Use clean semantic `<table>` elements with `<caption>`, `<th scope="col">`, and `<th scope="row">` for curriculum, fee comparisons, and batch timings (search engines extract table snippets readily).

### Anti-Fabrication Principles:
- `reviewCount` and `ratingCount` remain strictly omitted unless verified Google review API feeds are integrated.
- `validFrom` and schedule dates are omitted unless exact official batch dates are confirmed by the owner.
- Pricing in Schema must match the visible fee on the page with 100% exactitude.
