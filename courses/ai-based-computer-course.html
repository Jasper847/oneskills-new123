Frequently Asked Questions

Actionable answers about curriculum, timing, hardware access, and certifications.

Can complete beginners join with zero computer knowledge?

Yes. The course is built specifically for complete beginners. We start with turning on a computer, holding a mouse, understanding CPU and RAM components, and typing before moving forward.

What is the class schedule and timing?

Classes take place exclusively on weekends (Saturday and Sunday) from 11:00 AM to 1:00 PM for 3 months (12 weekends). Perfect for school students and working professionals.

What is the tuition fee and refund guarantee?

Tuition is PKR 18,000 for the full 3-month program. OneSkills provides a 14-day 100% money-back guarantee: if you attend the first two weeks and feel unsatisfied, your tuition is refunded in full.

Do I need to bring my own laptop to campus?

No. Every student receives a dedicated Intel Core i7 dual-monitor workstation in our air-conditioned lab with commercial generator backup power.

Where is the OneSkills campus in Sheikhupura?

We are centrally located on Lahore–Sargodha Road, directly opposite Mian Hospital and near KIPS College in Sheikhupura. Very convenient and easily accessible.

How do I book a free demo session before enrolling?

Send a message on WhatsApp to +92 317 603 6127. We will reserve your workstation for a live weekend demo session where you can experience the interactive lab environment.
