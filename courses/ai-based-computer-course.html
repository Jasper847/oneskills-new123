Course Blueprint

12 Weeks · 9 Hands-On Office Modules

From basic PC anatomy to AI-automated corporate spreadsheet modeling.

Week 1

Computer Hardware Architecture, Windows 11 & System Maintenance

Sessions 1–3
  • PC architecture: understanding CPU, RAM, SSD/HDD storage, motherboards, and peripheral connections.
  • Windows 11 navigation: file hierarchy, directory organization, zip archives, and cloud drive sync.
  • Essential maintenance: antivirus protection, malware defense, disk cleanup, and troubleshooting driver errors.
Core Skill: Confident Personal Computer Operating Practical Exercise: Complete Windows 11 optimization & backup audit
Week 2

English & Urdu Touch Typing Mastery (30–40 WPM Benchmark)

Sessions 4–6
  • Ergonomic posture, home row finger positioning (ASDF - JKL;), and blind touch-typing techniques without looking at the keyboard.
  • Daily timed typing drills with instant accuracy metrics and error rate tracking.
  • Urdu phonetic typing configuration in Windows 11 and InPage basics for local office documentation.
Target Metric: $\ge 30$ WPM with 95%+ Accuracy Practical Defense: 10-minute certified typing speed exam
Weeks 3–4

Microsoft Word 365: Executive Document Design, Tables & Formal Letters

Sessions 7–12
  • Paragraph formatting: line spacing, margins, tab stops, bullet styling, and heading styles (H1-H4).
  • Advanced tables, borders, cell shading, headers, footers, page numbering, and automated Table of Contents.
  • Mail Merge: generating 100+ personalized formal letters, certificates, and envelopes in one click.
Tools: Microsoft Word 365, Mail Merge Project: Multi-page corporate business proposal & formal letter package
Weeks 5–6

Microsoft Excel 365 Core: Formulas, Formatting & Data Validation

Sessions 13–18
  • Worksheet architecture: cells, ranges, relative vs absolute referencing ($A$1 locking).
  • Core mathematical and logical formulas: SUM, AVERAGE, COUNT, COUNTA, IF, AND, OR.
  • Conditional formatting, data validation dropdown lists, sorting, and multi-level data filtering.
Tools: Microsoft Excel 365 Project: Automated retail shop sales & expense tracking workbook
Weeks 7–8

Advanced Excel Analytics: VLOOKUP, XLOOKUP, Pivot Tables & Charts

Sessions 19–24
  • Lookup functions: mastering VLOOKUP, HLOOKUP, XLOOKUP, and INDEX/MATCH across multiple worksheets.
  • Pivot Tables and Pivot Charts: summarizing thousands of raw records into executive visual reports in seconds.
  • Building interactive dashboards with slicers, timeline filters, and automated summary KPIs.
Advanced Analytics: Corporate Office Requirement Project: Complete employee payroll & performance dashboard
Week 9

Microsoft PowerPoint 365 & Canva: Executive Presentations

Sessions 25–27
  • Presentation slide design: visual hierarchy, color palettes, infographics, and typography rules.
  • Master Slides (Slide Master), custom layouts, seamless slide transitions, and subtle object animations.
  • Exporting slide decks to interactive PDFs, video walkthroughs, and executive handouts.
Tools: PowerPoint 365, Canva Pro Project: 15-slide corporate company profile presentation
Week 10

Cloud Collaboration: Google Workspace, Drive & Email Etiquette

Sessions 28–30
  • Google Drive: file permissions, sharing links, version history, and real-time document co-authoring.
  • Professional email etiquette: formatting subject lines, CC/BCC protocols, professional signatures, and attachments.
  • Google Forms & Sheets: creating online surveys, customer feedback forms, and automatic responses.
Cloud Workspace: Google Docs, Sheets, Drive, Gmail Practical Exercise: Online customer inquiry form linked to Google Sheets
Week 11

Office Automation with Microsoft Copilot AI & ChatGPT

Sessions 31–33
  • Microsoft 365 Copilot: generating instant Word report drafts, formatting presentations, and summarizing documents.
  • Using ChatGPT for complex Excel formulas: describing what you want and letting AI write the exact nested formula.
  • Automated meeting notes, translation, email drafting, and PDF document summarization.
AI Productivity: Copilot in Word, Excel & PowerPoint Practical Exercise: Draft a 10-page report and summary deck using AI in 15 minutes
Week 12

Online Data Entry, Virtual Assistant Freelancing & Final Exam

Sessions 34–36
  • Setting up basic Fiverr gigs for Data Entry, Excel Formatting, PDF to Word conversion, and Virtual Assistant tasks.
  • CV and resume building in Microsoft Word, interview preparation for local office clerk jobs in Sheikhupura.

Common Questions About the Computer Course

Is this course suitable for beginners with zero computer background?

Yes. This course is specifically designed for Matric and Intermediate graduates, school students, and office job seekers who are starting from scratch. We teach everything from mouse handling, keyboard touch typing, and Windows 11 file management to advanced spreadsheet formulas.

How does OneSkills' computer course differ from traditional 2010 typing institutes?

Traditional institutes still teach outdated Windows 7 and basic typing on obsolete hardware. OneSkills modernizes your skillset with Windows 11, Microsoft 365 Copilot AI, advanced Excel analytics (VLOOKUP, XLOOKUP, Pivot Tables), professional Canva presentations, and cloud collaboration tools required by modern employers.

Do I need to bring my own laptop to class?

Yes. Students must bring their own laptops. OneSkills Academy does not provide dedicated individual computers. Practicing on your personal machine ensures your typing software, Microsoft Office suite, and custom templates remain configured permanently for home use.

Will I receive a course completion certificate?

Yes. Upon completing the 12-week course and passing the practical typing and office project evaluations, students receive a verified Certificate of Completion recognized for corporate and private office employment.

Can this course help me earn through online data entry freelancing?

Yes. Module 9 specifically covers online freelance micro-skills: data entry, Excel data cleaning, PDF to Word conversion, and virtual assistant tasks on Fiverr and Upwork, providing immediate earning potential.
