Common Questions About AI Tools at OneSkills

Do I need coding or technical skills to take this AI tools course?

No. This course requires zero coding. You will learn to use natural language prompt engineering, visual node-based automation platforms like Make.com and Zapier, and generative AI media suites (Midjourney, Kling, ElevenLabs) that operate without writing a single line of code.

What AI tools will I learn to use practically?

You will master 25+ cutting-edge tools including ChatGPT-4o, Claude 3.5 Sonnet, NotebookLM, Perplexity AI, Midjourney v6, Flux, Ideogram, Kling AI video, Runway Gen-3, ElevenLabs audio, Make.com, Zapier, Airtable, and CapCut AI.

How can I make money with AI tools after completing the course?

You will learn 3 monetization paths: 1) Launching faceless monetized YouTube and TikTok channels with automated AI scripts, visuals, and voiceovers; 2) Selling AI prompt engineering and creative production services on Upwork and Fiverr; and 3) Building custom AI customer service chatbots and lead capture automations for local businesses in Sheikhupura on monthly retainers.

Do I need to bring my own laptop to class?

Yes. Students must bring their own laptops. OneSkills Academy does not provide dedicated individual computers. Operating on your personal machine ensures your AI accounts, API keys, custom prompt libraries, and automation workflows stay with you for commercial use.

Is there an internship opportunity after completing the course?

Yes. After completing the course, students can gain practical experience through a one-month unpaid internship at OneSkills Academy. The internship period may be extended up to three months, depending on available projects and learning progression.

What is the tuition fee and refund guarantee policy?

Tuition is PKR 20,000 for the full 8-week on-campus program with flexible payment options (2 installments of PKR 10,000). Students may request a full refund within 14 calendar days of their fee payment date.
