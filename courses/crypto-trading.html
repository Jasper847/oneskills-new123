COMPREHENSIVE SYLLABUS

8-Module Crypto Trading & Market Structure Syllabus

72.5 hours of physical lab drills, moving step by step from exchange security to institutional order blocks and algorithmic AI bots.

  • Setting up secure workstations: TradingView, CoinMarketCap, CoinGecko, and hardware 2FA keys.
  • How crypto markets operate: Bitcoin dominance (BTC.D), market capitalization tiers, liquidity pools, and order books.
  • Fundamental Analysis: Tokenomics, circulating vs total supply, investor unlock cliffs, and project team credibility.
  • Smart Money vs Retail Behavior: Why retail traders buy tops and panic-sell bottoms—and how institutions trap liquidity.
  • Safe exchange operations: Navigating Binance/Bybit Spot and safe P2P bank deposit/withdrawal procedures in Pakistan.
  • Candlestick anatomy: Understanding buyer/seller pressure, wick rejections, and multi-timeframe pattern confirmation.
  • TradingView Mastery: Configuring institutional workspaces, clean watchlist hierarchies, and real-time price alerts.
  • Volume Analysis: Detecting true buying vs selling volume, volume climax spikes, and volume-price divergences.
  • Market Cycle Phases: Wyckoff accumulation, uptrend markup, institutional distribution, and markdown markdown traps.
  • Deliverable 01: Configure your professional multi-timeframe TradingView workspace and watchlist.
  • Deep Market Structure: Break of Structure (BOS), Change of Character (CHoCH), and HTF-to-LTF execution alignment.
  • Modern Order Block Technique: Validating true institutional supply/demand order blocks vs retail support/resistance traps.
  • Volume Spread Analysis (VSA): Spotting "No Demand" bars, stopping volume, and test bars at key order block zones.
  • Liquidity Gap Bars (FVG) & Liquidity Sweeps: Trading retail stop-loss hunts and equal highs/lows reversals.
  • Fibonacci Retracement: Pinpointing the Golden Pocket (0.618–0.786) and institutional take-profit targets.
  • Position Sizing Formula: Calculating exact coin quantities based on Stop Loss distance and the strict 1% risk limit.
  • Spot vs Futures Risk Management: Isolated vs cross margin, liquidation price formulas, and avoiding leverage wipes.
  • Trading Psychology: Eradicating FOMO, revenge trading, impatience, and over-leveraging during volatile crypto news events.
  • Trade Journaling: Tracking win-rates, risk-to-reward ratios, and emotional discipline in your personal performance log.
  • Deliverable 02: Build your automated position size calculator and personal risk rulebook.
  • DEX Trading Mechanics: Swapping on Uniswap, PancakeSwap, and Jupiter (Solana) before centralized exchange listings.
  • Meme Coin Analysis: Using DexScreener and DEXTools to monitor liquidity pools, volume spikes, and smart-money buyers.
  • Scam & Rug-Pull Audits: Checking honeypots, mint functions, locked LP tokens, and developer wallets with TokenSniffer.
  • ICO / IDO / Launchpad basics: Evaluating early-stage token allocation rounds and calculating real risk-to-reward.
  • AI Crypto Bots: How grid bots, DCA bots, and AI market scanners work—and when to disable them during market crashes.
  • Pine Script Basics: Using modern AI to code custom TradingView indicators, multi-condition alerts, and backtesters.
  • Automating market research: Using AI to summarize macro economic news, CPI data, and token sentiment in minutes.
  • Deliverable 03: Code and deploy your custom AI-built indicator on TradingView.
  • Writing your Personal Trading Plan: Defining traded pairs, session windows, entry triggers, and daily loss stop rules.
  • Live market structure defense: Analyzing live charts in real time before mentors in the Trade Room.
  • Final Evaluation & Award of Official OneSkills Crypto Trading Completion Certificate.
