Frequently Asked Questions

Actionable answers regarding computer science foundations, prerequisites, tuition, and coding outcomes.

Why learn C++ and JavaScript together in one program?

C++ gives you deep structural understanding of memory pointers, CPU efficiency, and algorithms. JavaScript teaches event-driven architecture, asynchronous execution, and modern application development. Together, they create an exceptionally well-rounded software engineer.

Can complete beginners with no math or coding background join?

Yes. The curriculum starts from basic computational thinking, variables, conditionals, and loops before advancing into functions, arrays, memory pointers, and OOP. No prior coding experience is required.

What is the tuition fee and refund guarantee?

Tuition is PKR 32,000 for the full 16-week program. OneSkills provides a 14-day 100% money-back guarantee: if you attend the first two weeks and feel unsatisfied, your tuition is refunded in full.

Do I need to bring my own laptop to campus?

No. Every student receives a dedicated Intel Core i7 dual-monitor workstation in our air-conditioned lab with commercial backup generator power.

Where is the OneSkills campus in Sheikhupura?

We are centrally located on Lahore–Sargodha Road, directly opposite Mian Hospital and near KIPS College in Sheikhupura. Very convenient and easily accessible.
