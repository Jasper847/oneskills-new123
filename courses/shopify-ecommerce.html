COMPREHENSIVE SYLLABUS

8-Week Shopify & Dropshipping Roadmap

50+ hours of physical lab drills, moving step by step from wholesale supplier negotiation to multi-city courier automation.

  • Empirical product research: Using Meta Ad Library, TikTok Creative Center, and competitor spy tools to find high-demand SKUs.
  • Wholesale sourcing protocols: Visiting and negotiating with supplier networks in Lahore Shah Alam Market and Hall Road.
  • Unit Economics & Landed Margin Modeling: Calculating product cost, shipping weight, ad acquisition cost, and minimum 40% margin.
  • Supplier agreements, stock reservation terms, and credit arrangements for local Pakistani merchants.
  • Deliverable 01: Validated Product Hunting & Margin Feasibility Matrix for 5 vetted SKUs.
  • Shopify Online Store 2.0: Setting up custom product pages, high-converting hero sections, and collection grids.
  • Integrating 1-Click Cash on Delivery forms: Skipping complicated checkouts to match Pakistani customer buying habits.
  • Mobile Speed Optimization: Compressing WebP assets, minimizing JavaScript bloat, and achieving sub-2-second load times.
  • Custom Domain, SSL security certificates, and legal policy pages (Terms of Service, Refund Policy, Privacy Policy).
  • Deliverable 02: Live, fully functional mobile-optimized Shopify store ready for order processing.
  • UGC Video Creative Production: Scripting problem-solution hooks and editing high-retention video ads in CapCut Pro.
  • Meta Business Manager & TikTok Ads: Setting up Pixel, Server-Side Conversions API (CAPI), and custom audiences.
  • Campaign Architecture: Testing creative angles (CBO vs ABO), budget allocation rules, and optimizing Cost-Per-Purchase (CPP).
  • Scaling winning ad sets without fatigue: Horizontal lookalike scaling and dynamic creative testing.
  • Deliverable 03: Production-ready video ad suite with configured CAPI tracking and active test campaign.
  • Courier Portals: Connecting PostEx, Trax, and TCS API systems for automated bulk consignment note (CN) generation.
  • Deploying WhatsApp Order Verification bots: Confirming customer addresses before dispatch to slash returns below 10%.
  • Daily Cash Reconciliation: Tracking courier remittance cycles, disbursement ledgers, and preventing cash leaks.
  • Scaling to multi-product private label brands and introducing international dropshipping pipelines.
  • Deliverable 04: Fully automated courier fulfillment pipeline with WhatsApp RTO shield and daily ledger.
