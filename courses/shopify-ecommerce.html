Frequently Asked Questions

Concrete answers regarding product hunting, local Pakistani couriers, advertising budgets, and store ownership.

Do I need prior technical experience to build a Shopify store?

No previous coding or technical experience is needed. The Shopify course starts from zero: understanding store architecture, customizing Online Store 2.0 themes, and integrating payment and COD apps with step-by-step physical guidance.

How does product hunting work in Pakistan for local dropshipping?

We teach you how to analyze viral ad libraries on Facebook and TikTok, validate consumer demand, and source inventory directly from wholesale distributors in Lahore Shah Alam Market and Hall Road at rock-bottom prices.

How do we handle Cash on Delivery (COD) and couriers in Pakistan?

You will connect live accounts with Pakistan's top couriers including PostEx, Trax, and TCS. You will learn automated WhatsApp confirmation bots that reduce Return-to-Origin (RTO) below 10% and protect profit margins.

How much advertising budget is required to run test campaigns?

We recommend an initial test ad budget of PKR 10,000 to PKR 15,000 for running Meta and TikTok video ads. We teach you rigorous budget capping strategies so you never waste ad spend on unproven products.

What is the tuition fee and refund guarantee?

Tuition is PKR 25,000 for the full 10-week program. OneSkills provides a 14-day 100% money-back guarantee: if you attend the first two weeks and feel unsatisfied, your tuition is refunded in full.

How do I book a free demo session before enrolling?

Simply message us on WhatsApp at +92 317 603 6127. We will reserve your dedicated workstation for an upcoming live store building workshop.
