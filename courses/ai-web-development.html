Frequently Asked Questions

Concrete answers regarding hardware, prerequisites, job outcomes, and tuition policies.

Is this web development course suitable for complete beginners?

Yes. The program is built from the ground up: starting with semantic HTML5, modern CSS, and JavaScript fundamentals before advancing to React 19 and Next.js 15. Every student receives personal mentor attention inside our lab.

Do I need to bring my own laptop to class?

No laptop is required. You will code on a dedicated Intel Core i7 12th Gen workstation equipped with dual displays, 16GB RAM, high-speed NVMe storage, and uninterrupted power backup in our Sheikhupura computer lab.

How will learning AI tools help me as a web developer?

Modern engineering teams expect engineers who know how to build with AI. You will learn to use Cursor, Claude 3.5 Sonnet, and Vercel AI SDK to write code faster, debug issues, and embed streaming AI interfaces into production apps.

Can I get freelance clients or local software house jobs after graduating?

Yes. By the end of the 10 weeks, you will have built 5 live, deployed web applications on your personal domain, giving you an undeniable portfolio for Upwork, Fiverr, and software houses across Lahore and Sheikhupura.

What is the tuition fee and refund guarantee?

Tuition is PKR 28,000 for the full 10-week program with split-payment options available. OneSkills provides a 14-day 100% money-back guarantee: if you attend the first two weeks and feel unsatisfied, your tuition is refunded in full.

How do I book a free demo session before paying tuition?

Simply message our admissions desk on WhatsApp at +92 317 603 6127. We will reserve a workstation seat for you in an upcoming live coding workshop so you can inspect our campus and meet lead mentors.
