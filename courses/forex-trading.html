Frequently Asked Questions

Concrete answers regarding trading platforms, prop firm evaluations, tuition, and live execution.

Can complete beginners join the Forex Trading course in Sheikhupura?

Yes. The course begins with currency pair basics, pip values, and MetaTrader 5 navigation before advancing into institutional price action and funded account challenges. No prior trading experience is required.

What is SMC and how does it help retail traders?

Smart Money Concepts (SMC) reveals how institutional players build positions around key liquidity pools. Rather than chasing breakouts, you learn to enter at Order Blocks and Fair Value Gaps with tight, high-reward stop losses.

What is the tuition fee and refund policy?

Tuition is PKR 30,000 for the full 12-week program. OneSkills offers a 14-day 100% money-back guarantee: if you attend the first two weeks and feel unsatisfied, your tuition is refunded in full.

Yes. The entire final month is focused on prop firm evaluation simulations, managing daily drawdown ceilings (max 5%), and building the statistical consistency required to manage funded corporate trading capital.
