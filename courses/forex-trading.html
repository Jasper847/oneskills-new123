COMPREHENSIVE SYLLABUS

Complete 3-Month Forex & Price Action Syllabus

76 structured lessons across 12 weeks, matching live London and New York market sessions during class hours.

  • What is Forex Trading? Mechanics of the $7.5 Trillion/day global foreign exchange market.
  • Trading as a Business vs Gambling: Probability mindset and surviving the 90-90-90 retail trap.
  • Going Long (Buy) vs Going Short (Sell): Profiting in two-way currency movements.
  • Debunking "get rich quick" fake gurus, signal scams, and high-risk martingale EA bots.
  • Major Currency Pairs (EUR/USD, GBP/USD, USD/JPY) vs Commodity Pairs and Gold (XAU/USD).
  • The 3 Global Forex Sessions: Asian (Tokyo), European (London), and US (New York).
  • Best Trading Times in Pakistan (PKT): London Open (1:00 PM) and London-NY Overlap (6:00 PM – 10:00 PM).
  • Understanding spread expansion, rollover fees (swaps), and volatility spikes during market opens.
  • Measuring Price: Pips, Pipettes, and Point values on 5-decimal pairs and Gold.
  • Lot Sizes & Exposure: Standard (1.00), Mini (0.10), and Micro (0.01) lot calculations.
  • Leverage & Margin: Margin call, stop-out levels, and why uncontrolled leverage destroys accounts.
  • The 1% Maximum Risk Rule: Calculating exact position sizing before placing any market order.
  • Installing and setting up MetaTrader 4, MetaTrader 5, and TradingView on desktop laptops.
  • Order Types: Market Execution vs Pending Orders (Buy Limit, Sell Limit, Buy Stop, Sell Stop).
  • Non-Negotiable Protections: Pre-setting hard Stop-Loss (SL) and Take-Profit (TP) parameters.
  • Practical Assessment 01: Live test on platform execution, lot sizing, and session timing.
  • Candlestick anatomy: Reading real bodies vs wicks as buyer-seller pressure battles.
  • Higher Timeframe (HTF: Daily/H4) for market bias vs Lower Timeframe (LTF: M15/M5) for entries.
  • The Multi-Timeframe Top-Down Analysis workflow: Aligning macro trends with micro timing.
  • Pin Bars & Wick Rejections: Identifying institutional absorption at major liquidity zones.
  • Engulfing Bars (Bullish & Bearish): Momentum shift confirmations on key levels.
  • Doji & Inside Bars: Avoiding low-probability choppy consolidation periods.
  • The Golden Candle-Close Rule: Why waiting for the candle close eliminates 80% of fake entries.
  • Support & Resistance as Dynamic Zones rather than rigid horizontal lines.
  • Supply & Demand Logic: Identifying bank origin zones where massive unfilled orders rest.
  • Structural Swings: Higher Highs (HH) / Higher Lows (HL) vs Lower Highs (LH) / Lower Lows (LL).
  • Break of Structure (BOS) vs Change of Character (CHoCH): Detecting genuine trend reversals.
  • Liquidity Sweeps & Stop Hunts: How retail breakout traders get trapped by institutional players.
  • Practical Assessment 02: Live test on naked chart structure marking and bias verification.
  • Why indicators fail on their own: Using 50/200 EMAs and RSI purely as secondary confluence.
  • Spotting Regular & Hidden RSI Divergences at key Higher Timeframe supply/demand zones.
  • Trading Around High-Impact News: Using Forex Factory for CPI, NFP, and FOMC rate decisions.
  • Strategy 01 — Trend Bounce: Pullbacks to key moving averages and structure levels.
  • Strategy 02 — Break & Retest: Entering confirmed structure retests with tight stops.
  • Strategy 03 — Range Fakeout: Trading liquidity sweeps at session highs and lows.
  • Strategy 04 — Supply/Demand Sniper: Entering fresh institutional origin order blocks.
  • Strategy 05 — Order Block & FVG (SMC): Combining Fair Value Gaps and order block mitigation.
  • The Mathematics of Consistency: Why a 40% win rate generates profits with 1:2 and 1:3 R:R setups.
  • Trader Psychology: Conquering revenge trading, greed, fear of missing out (FOMO), and hesitation.
  • Mandatory 30-Trade Performance Journaling: Tracking entry, exit, screenshot, and emotional state.
  • Transition roadmap: Moving safely from Demo to Micro-Lot (0.01) live capital.
  • Prop Firm Evaluation Preparation: Managing 5% daily / 10% total max drawdown on funded accounts (FTMO, FundedNext).
  • Final Evaluation: Defense of audited 30-trade journal and personal Trading Plan.
  • Graduation Ceremony & Award of Official OneSkills Forex Trading Certificate.
