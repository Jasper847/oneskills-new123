COMPREHENSIVE SYLLABUS

12-Week Web3, Blockchain & Airdrop Curriculum

67 lessons and 80+ hours of physical lab drills, combining foundational cryptography with daily on-chain execution.

  • What is Blockchain Technology? Blocks, nodes, consensus (Proof of Work vs Proof of Stake), and immutability.
  • Web1 vs Web2 vs Web3: Evolution from platform-owned databases to user-owned decentralized protocols.
  • How decentralization works in practice and why modern fintech is transitioning on-chain.
  • Real-world institutional use cases: stablecoins, cross-border settlements, and decentralized identities.
  • Web3 career opportunities in Pakistan: researcher, community moderator, ambassador, and ecosystem contributor.
  • Custodial vs Non-Custodial Wallets: CEX accounts vs Hot Wallets (MetaMask, Rabby, Phantom) and Hardware cold storage (Ledger, Trezor).
  • Step-by-step setup in class: private key cryptography, 12/24-word seed recovery phrases, and physical backup hygiene.
  • The 3-Wallet Isolation System: separating your Vault Wallet, Mainnet Airdrop Wallet, and Burner/Testnet Wallet.
  • Identifying phishing links, fake X/Twitter support bots, Discord fake announcements, and address poisoning attacks.
  • What are Decentralized Applications (dApps) and how browser extensions interact with smart contracts.
  • Understanding Gas Fees, Gwei, base fee vs priority tip, and executing transactions during optimal low-gas windows.
  • Layer-1 vs Layer-2: Exploring Ethereum Mainnet, Arbitrum, Optimism, Base, zkSync, Scroll, Linea, and Solana.
  • Adding custom RPC endpoints to MetaMask/Rabby for high-speed, reliable transaction broadcast.
  • Automated Market Makers (AMMs) vs order books: swapping on Uniswap, Jupiter, and PancakeSwap.
  • Managing slippage tolerance, front-running MEV protection, and understanding liquidity price impact.
  • Cross-Chain Bridges: Moving tokens between Ethereum, L2 networks, and Solana via Stargate, Across, Jumper, and Orbiter.
  • Using DEX Aggregators (1inch, Odos, DefiLlama Swap) to find the cheapest routing across fragmented pools.
  • Tokenomics analysis: circulating vs total supply, token unlock cliffs, inflation curves, and utility architecture.
  • Researching Venture Capital backing: using CryptoRank, RootData, and Dropstab to track Tier-1 funds (a16z, Paradigm, Binance Labs).
  • GitHub commits audit, developer activity, Discord engagement health, and identifying bot-inflated hype.
  • Red-flag checklists: unverified contracts, honeypots, anonymous founders with no track record, and suspicious mint rights.
  • Decentralized Lending & Borrowing: Collateralized debt positions, liquidation health factor, and protocols like Aave.
  • Liquid Staking & Restaking: Exploring Lido, EigenLayer, and Ether.fi mechanics.
  • Liquidity Pools (LPs): Providing liquidity, collecting swap fees, and calculating Impermanent Loss risk.
  • DeFi Risk Management: Smart contract audit verification, protocol TVL analysis on DefiLlama, and stablecoin de-peg risks.
  • Non-Fungible Tokens (NFTs): ERC-721 vs ERC-1155 standards, metadata IPFS pinning, and provenance verification.
  • On-Chain Proof-of-Participation badges: claiming Galxe OATs, Guild pins, and Gitcoin Passport humanity verification.
  • Navigating NFT Marketplaces (OpenSea, Magic Eden, Tensor) and recognizing wash trading vs genuine market depth.
  • Avoiding malicious NFT airdrops sent to your public wallet address without ever approving contract interactions.
  • The Mechanics of Token Distributions: Historical case studies (Uniswap, Arbitrum, Celestia, Hyperliquid, Jito).
  • Zero-Capital Testnet Farming: Claiming testnet faucets (Sepolia, Holesky) and interacting with testnet dApps at $0 cost.
  • Mainnet Retroactive Strategies: Volume tiers, contract interaction frequency, liquidity bridging, and points systems.
  • Anti-Sybil Architecture: Understanding how protocols cluster and disqualify bot farms; maintaining human wallet footprints.
  • Creating and managing your personalized Notion/Excel Airdrop Tracker with milestone alarms.
  • Web3 Social Landscape: Configuring professional Web3 profiles on X (Twitter), Discord, Warpcast, and Telegram.
  • Participating in InfoFi campaigns: Kaito Yaps, Galxe Quests, Zealy Leaderboards, and Layer3 Cubes.
  • Acquiring Discord Roles: OG, Early Tester, Community Contributor, and Ambassador roles through genuine contribution.
  • Zero-Capital monetization: How students earn grants, bounties, and rewards by beta-testing new protocol software.
  • Investigative Block Explorer Reading: Inspecting Etherscan, Solscan, Arbiscan, and Basescan transactions.
  • Smart Money & Whale Tracking: Using DeBank, Arkham Intelligence, and Zerion to monitor top VC and founder wallets.
  • Analyzing smart contract liquidity locks, vesting schedules, and token holder concentration metrics.
  • Automated Contract Scanners: Checking honeypots, malicious transfer taxes, and blacklist logic with TokenSniffer.
  • How Smart Contracts Work: Verified vs Unverified contracts on blockchain explorers.
  • Token Approvals vs Token Transfers: Understanding why unlimited allowance approvals leave wallets vulnerable forever.
