Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers regarding wallets, testnet funding, tuition, and earning mechanics.

What is Web3 and why is it worth learning today?

Web3 is the next internet paradigm where financial value and digital assets are owned directly by users via cryptographic keys rather than held inside centralized databases. Learning Web3 unlocks high-paying global remote roles and early protocol rewards.

How does crypto airdrop farming work?

Emerging blockchain networks and DeFi protocols distribute free governance tokens to early active users who bridge funds and interact with smart contracts. We teach you how to systematically qualify for these allocations safely without sybil disqualification.

Tuition is PKR 28,000 for the full 12-week program. OneSkills provides a 14-day 100% money-back guarantee: if you attend the first two weeks and feel unsatisfied, your tuition is refunded in full.

No. Most initial tasks are executed on zero-cost Testnet faucets, and Mainnet exercises focus on ultra-cheap Layer-2 rollups (Arbitrum, Base, Optimism) where gas fees are pennies ($0.05 – $0.30).
