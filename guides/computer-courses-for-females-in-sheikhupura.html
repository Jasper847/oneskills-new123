Top 3 Work-From-Home Skills for Females in Sheikhupura

1. AI Graphic Design & Social Media Creatives

Graphic design is one of the most popular freelance tracks for women because it allows you to work completely on your own schedule from a home laptop. You learn how to use Canva Pro and Midjourney to create Instagram posts, Pinterest pins, wedding e-cards, and promotional banners for local brands and international clients on Fiverr.

2. AI-Based Office Management & Virtual Assistance

International business owners constantly hire remote Virtual Assistants (VAs) from Pakistan for $5 to $12 per hour to manage their email inboxes, organize Excel client records, draft correspondence with ChatGPT, and format Word documents. Our 12-weekend AI Computer course teaches you every single tool required for these roles.

3. Shopify Store Management & Digital Marketing

Pakistani women are leading some of the fastest-growing clothing, jewelry, and cosmetics brands on Instagram. In this track, you learn how to set up your own online store or manage social media advertising campaigns (Meta Ads) for e-commerce businesses from the comfort of your room.
