01 What programs do you offer at OneSkills Academy?
+ −

We offer 8 in-demand programs designed for real skills and real opportunities:

  • Freelancing & Digital Marketing
  • Forex Trading
  • AI Tools & Automation
  • Web3 & Blockchain
  • AI Powered Web Development
  • Programming JavaScript & C++
  • Crypto Trading
  • Computer & Office Management

Each program is practical, project-based and taught by experienced instructors with hands-on lab workstations.

02 Do I need any prior experience to join the courses?
+ −

No prior experience is necessary. Every course starts with fundamental concepts and uses step-by-step practical screen-sharing. Our instructors guide you through software setups, AI tools, and real-time execution in the lab.

03 What are the fees? Are installment options available?
+ −

Our fee structures are designed to be accessible for students and job seekers in Sheikhupura, offering flexible monthly installment plans.

Ask about current fees on WhatsApp →
04 Are classes conducted on-campus or online?
+ −

Main flagship batches are held live on-campus with hands-on computer workstations, high-speed backup power, and direct mentor supervision at our Sheikhupura campus (Lahore-Sargodha Road, Opp. Mian Hospital). Hybrid and remote support options are also available for follow-up practice at home.

05 How many students are in a batch?
+ −

We strictly cap our cohorts at 10–12 students maximum. This ensures every student gets their own workstation, individual review on assignments, and direct 1-on-1 guidance from the master trainer.

06 When can I start applying my skills to real opportunities?
+ −

Students begin working on practical projects, client-facing profiles, proposals and portfolio work during training. The time it takes to secure opportunities varies by skill track, consistency, market conditions and individual execution.

07 Do you provide certificates after course completion?
+ −

Yes. Students who successfully complete attendance standards and final portfolio project submissions receive an official Course Completion Certificate, along with lifetime alumni community and mentorship access.

08 What is the admission process & how do I book a free demo class?
+ −

You can reserve your demo seat in 30 seconds via WhatsApp. Attend a live class on campus, meet the instructors, inspect the learning environment, and decide if the program is right for you before paying any fees.
