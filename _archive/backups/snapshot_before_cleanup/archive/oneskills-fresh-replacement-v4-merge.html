06 FAQ

The questions people ask before they say yes.

Short answers, no fluff. If anything is still unclear, the demo class is where we go deeper.

?How much does OneSkills cost?
Fees vary by program length and support level. Ask on WhatsApp for the current fee, installment options, and the next demo class date.
?Do I need prior experience?
No prior experience is required for the beginner tracks. We start from fundamentals and move into guided practice quickly.
?Are classes on-campus or online?
Classes are primarily on-campus in Sheikhupura. Remote support is available for follow-up help, but the main sessions are in person.
?Will I get a certificate?
Yes. Students who complete the attendance and project requirements receive a completion certificate.
?Can I really earn after the course?
The curriculum is built around portfolio work, client simulation, and earning-focused skills. Results vary, but the goal is to help you become client-ready, not just course-complete.
?How can I book a demo?
Use the WhatsApp button or the demo class CTA to reserve your spot. If seats are full, we’ll move you to the next available batch.
?How many students are in each batch?
Batches are intentionally small so students get direct mentor attention and feedback.
