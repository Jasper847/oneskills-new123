Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026 · OneSkills Academy, Sheikhupura

Information We Collect

OneSkills Academy collects only essential information required for student enrollment, demo class scheduling, and official academic communication. This includes your full name, phone/WhatsApp number, email address, educational background, and preferred training course track.

How We Use Your Information

We strictly utilize the information provided to deliver educational services, support learning, and maintain smooth academy operations:

  • Enrollment & Demos: To process admissions, confirm seat reservations, and schedule free demo sessions.
  • Class Coordination: To communicate lecture schedules, batch updates, lab access guidelines, and urgent notifications.
  • Program Improvement: To evaluate curriculum feedback and optimize training methodology.
  • Direct Support: To respond to inquiries submitted via WhatsApp, phone, or website contact forms.

Data Protection & Security

We do not sell, rent, trade, or distribute your personal information to third-party marketing networks or external vendors. All student records are stored securely with restricted administrative access and are used exclusively for legitimate OneSkills Academy operations.
