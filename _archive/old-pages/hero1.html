We offer 6 high-income skill programs: Forex & Crypto Master Trading, AI Tools & Workflow Automation, Freelancing & Digital Marketing, Full-Stack Web Development (React & AI), YouTube Automation & Content Creation, and Web3 & Blockchain Airdrops.

Our fee structures are designed to be accessible for students and job seekers in Sheikhupura, offering flexible monthly installment plans. Reach out to our admissions desk on WhatsApp for the exact fee breakdown, discount vouchers for early enrollment, and upcoming batch seat availability.
04 Do I need prior experience to join?
No prior experience is necessary. Every course starts with fundamental concepts and uses step-by-step practical screen-sharing. Our instructors guide you through software setups, AI tools, and real-time execution in the lab.
05 Are classes on-campus or online?
Yes! All flagship batches are held live on-campus with hands-on computer lab workstations, high-speed backup power, and direct in-person instructor supervision. We also provide remote screen-share support for follow-up questions at home.

We strictly cap our batches at 12 students maximum. This ensures every student gets their own workstation, individual review on their assignments, and 1-on-1 time with the master trainer.

Our curriculum is 100% earning-first. By week 4–6, students build live Fiverr/Upwork profiles, send audited client proposals, or execute disciplined demo trading. Many students land their first local or global client orders within 60–90 days of dedicated execution.
08 Do I get a certificate and lifetime support?
Yes. Graduates receive a verified Course Completion Certificate and gain permanent access to our private WhatsApp alumni community, weekly live trading analysis rooms, and ongoing client review mentorship.
