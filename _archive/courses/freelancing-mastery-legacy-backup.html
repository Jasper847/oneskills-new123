Frequently Asked Questions

Concrete answers regarding client bidding, international payment settlements, tuition, and hardware.

Can I start freelancing if I do not have a prior technical degree?

Yes. Freelance clients hire based on verified project proof, clear communication, and problem-solving rather than academic degrees. We teach you how to package your existing strengths into high-demand services.

How does OneSkills help me win my first client?

You participate in supervised live bidding labs inside our Sheikhupura campus. Mentors review your profile copy, proposal drafts, portfolio tear-sheets, and interview scripts before you submit them to international clients.

How do Pakistani freelancers receive payments from international clients?

We guide you step-by-step through setting up Payoneer, direct local bank wire transfers (IBAN), and Wise accounts, ensuring you receive foreign remittances legally with zero withheld funds.

What is the difference between Upwork, Fiverr, and LinkedIn outreach?

Upwork is ideal for high-ticket hourly and fixed contracts; Fiverr is optimized for productized services; and LinkedIn outreach allows you to bypass platform commissions entirely by pitching founders directly.

What is the tuition fee and refund guarantee?

Tuition is PKR 25,000 for the full 12-week program with split-payment options available. OneSkills provides a 14-day 100% money-back guarantee: if you attend the first two weeks and feel unsatisfied, your tuition is refunded in full.

How do I book a free demo session before enrolling?

Simply message our admissions desk on WhatsApp at +92 317 603 6127. We will reserve a workstation seat for you in an upcoming live client pitch teardown session so you can inspect our campus and meet lead mentors.
