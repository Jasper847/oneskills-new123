02 What is the exact fee structure, and are monthly installments available? +
The complete 3-month flagship program (36 live lab sessions) is PKR 24,000 (one-time payment) or PKR 8,500/month across 3 monthly installments. Every student gets a free live demo class before enrollment plus a 2-week satisfaction guarantee.

We provide a dedicated computer workstation with high-speed internet and power backup for every single student in our 12-seat lab. If you also have a personal laptop, you are welcome to bring it so our mentors can configure your software and freelance accounts directly on your machine.

Yes! Our campus is situated right on Main Lahore–Sargodha Road (Opposite Mian Hospital, Near KIPS College), Sheikhupura's prime educational corridor. You create and optimize your Fiverr gigs and Upwork profile during Weeks 1–2, and continuously add 4 verifiable portfolio case studies (SEO audit, WordPress/Shopify landing page, Meta Ad campaign, Google Ads blueprint) over Weeks 3–12 so you are actively pitching clients while still under mentor supervision.

Yes. Upon completing your 4 portfolio deliverables, you receive an official OneSkills Academy Completion Certificate along with lifetime access to our alumni mentorship community—so when you land your first client order or ad campaign, our mentors help you deliver it successfully.
