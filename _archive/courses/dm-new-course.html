Can I learn Digital Marketing and Freelancing if I have no technical background?

Yes. This course is designed for complete beginners. You do not need coding skills or an IT degree. How does this course help me earn through both local and international clients?

You learn two distinct monetization channels: 1) International freelancing in USD on Upwork, Fiverr, and LinkedIn for high-ticket projects; and 2) Monthly marketing retainers (PKR 30,000 to PKR 80,000/month per client) managing social media, Google Maps, and Meta ads for local businesses in Sheikhupura, Lahore, and across Pakistan.

What modern AI marketing tools are taught in this course?

You will master ChatGPT and Claude for direct-response ad copywriting, audience research, and SEO briefs; Canva Magic Studio and Midjourney for visual creatives and ad banners; and CapCut AI for editing viral Reels, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts video ads.

How do Pakistani students receive payments from international clients?

We guide you step-by-step through setting up Payoneer and Wise accounts linked directly to your local Pakistani bank account (IBAN) for zero-withheld USD transfers, ensuring full legal compliance with foreign remittance documentation, as well as JazzCash, EasyPaisa, and bank transfers for local clients.
